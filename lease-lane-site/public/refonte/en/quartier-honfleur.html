    Town profile

    Renting an apartment in Honfleur.

    Honfleur occupies a hilly area of the Appalachian foothills, between Saint-Gervais to the north, Saint-Lazare-de-Bellechasse to the southeast and Sainte-Claire to the southwest. The Boyer River has its source there. The territory covers about 51 km².

    In the 2021 census, Honfleur had 881 residents. Agriculture and forestry work long supported the population, and Maurice Laliberté’s farm received the Mérite agricole gold medal in 1962. The Fleur de Lys hall, owned by the municipality, can hold up to 290 people.

    • Housing

      Mostly single-family houses and farms, with few rental buildings.

    • Nearby

      Salle Fleur de Lys · Bibliothèque La Livrothèque · Saint-Anselme

    • Good to know

      Honfleur lies between Routes 279 and 277, about 9 km from Saint-Anselme. The Bellechasse RCM offers on-demand public transit to Lévis, Montmagny and Sainte-Marie.

    History

    The history of Honfleur.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1903

    3. 1905

    4. 1915

      Incorporation of the municipality

    5. 2004

      Parish centennial

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1850

      //

      The territory of Honfleur first belonged to the parishes of Saint-Anselme, Saint-Gervais, Saint-Lazare and Sainte-Claire. The idea of founding a separate parish there dates back to the late 1850s. It divided the residents of Bellechasse for a long time.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1903 – 1904

      //

      In 1903, the archbishop authorized the construction of a church and a rectory, even though the parish did not yet have official status. The post office opened the same year took the name Honfleur. The church, built by Elzéar Métivier, held its first Mass at Christmas 1904.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1905 – 1915

      //

      The canonical erection of the parish of Notre-Dame-du-Bon-Conseil was decreed in 1905. Rome confirmed it in 1907 and asked that the municipality not be created until opposition had ended. It was not incorporated until 1915, which makes Honfleur the youngest municipality in the Bellechasse RCM.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 1928 – 2004

      //

      The church’s interior finishing was completed in 1928. In the 1930s, two cheese factories opened and, by the end of the decade, residents were operating 75 sugar bushes. A Desjardins caisse populaire opened in 1942, and the parish celebrated its centennial in 2004.

    01Where is Honfleur?

    In the heart of the Bellechasse RCM, in Chaudière-Appalaches, about 9 km northeast of Saint-Anselme. The municipality is bordered by Saint-Gervais, Saint-Lazare-de-Bellechasse and Sainte-Claire.

    02Where does the name Honfleur come from?

    It recalls Honfleur, the Normandy port from which Champlain sailed in 1603. According to the Commission de toponymie, it also evokes Saint-Léonard-de-Honfleur, the place of origin of the ancestor of Cardinal Louis-Nazaire Bégin.

    03What is there in the village of Honfleur?

    Église Notre-Dame-du-Bon-Conseil, built in 1904, the La Livrothèque library and the Fleur de Lys hall, which can hold up to 290 people.

    Town profile

    Renting an apartment in Saint-Pierre-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud.

    Saint-Pierre-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud is a parish municipality of the Côte-du-Sud, between Montmagny and Saint-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud. The Rivière du Sud runs along its northern boundary and makes the land in this area fertile. The Morigeau and des Poitras rivers and the ruisseau de la Blague complete the river network.

    Agriculture and maple production occupy a large place, and the municipality presents itself as the cradle of Québec’s well diggers. An astronomy club set up there in 2006, and an observation site equipped with observatories, the Portail étoilé, was inaugurated in the forest in 2011.

    • Housing

      Mostly single-family homes and farms, with a few units in the village.

    • Nearby

      Astronomical observation park · Bibliothèque La Volumineuse · Centre récréatif Groupe Picard

    • Good to know

      Route 228 runs through the municipality and reaches Montmagny and Autoroute 20. The astronomical observation park is in the forest, about ten kilometres from the village.

    History

    The history of Saint-Pierre-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1713

      Construction of the first chapel

    2. 1776

      Battle of Saint-Pierre

    3. 1785

      Opening of the current church

    4. 1855

      Creation of the parish municipality

    5. 2013

      300th anniversary of the parish

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1646 – 1713

      //

      The territory is part of the seigneury of Rivière-du-Sud, granted to Charles Huault de Montmagny in 1646. A man named Lecolombier briefly settled there in 1696, but colonization began mainly in the early 18th century. In 1709, Pierre Blanchet donated a piece of land, and a chapel was built in 1713 north of the Rivière du Sud.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1722 – 1776

      //

      The parish, whose boundaries were set in 1722, was placed under the protection of Saint Peter in honour of Pierre Blanchet. First served by priests from Saint-Thomas, it received a resident priest in 1751. On March 25, 1776, during the siege of Québec by the Continental Army, the Battle of Saint-Pierre took place near the village between two groups made up mostly of Canadian militiamen.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1784 – 1972

      //

      The current church, the parish’s third, was built in 1784 and 1785 on the model proposed by Bishop Jean-Olivier Briand. Thomas Baillairgé redesigned its steeple in 1839. It was classified in 1972.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 1845 – 2013

      //

      The municipality was created in 1845, abolished in 1847 then reinstated in 1855, and the parish was canonically erected in 1848. The post office, opened in 1852, bore the name Saint-Pierre-Montmagny. The cemetery, laid out from 1908 in terraces on a rock outcrop, is now a designated heritage site. In 2013, the parish celebrated its 300th anniversary.

    01Where is Saint-Pierre-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud?

    On the South Shore, in the MRC de Montmagny in Chaudière-Appalaches, between Montmagny and Saint-François-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud. The Rivière du Sud runs along the north of its territory.

    02What happened at the Battle of Saint-Pierre?

    On March 25, 1776, during the siege of Québec by the Continental Army, pro-American forces routed Loyalist militiamen near the village. Both sides were made up largely of Canadian militiamen.

    03Which notable people were born in Saint-Pierre-de-la-Rivière-du-Sud?

    Abbé Maurice Proulx, a pioneer of documentary film in Québec, was born there in 1902. Augustin-Magloire Blanchet, born in 1797, became Bishop of Walla Walla, then of Nesqually, now the Archdiocese of Seattle.

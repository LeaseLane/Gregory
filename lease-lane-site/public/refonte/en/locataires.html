    01What is an emergency, and which number should I call?

    A major water leak, a heating failure in winter, a power outage, a door or lock that no longer closes, a faulty smoke detector. Call 418-473-3208 at any time; in case of danger, call 911.

    02How do I submit a work request and follow its progress?

    Submit the request in your tenant portal, with photos. You receive a tracking number and an appointment window, then a notice at each stage: received, scheduled, in progress, completed.

    03How do I pay my rent?

    In your tenant portal or by transfer. You receive an automatic receipt as soon as the payment arrives.

    04How do I assign my lease or sublet my unit?

    Send a written notice from your tenant portal, with the contact details of the proposed person. Since Bill 31, the owner can accept, refuse for a serious reason or, for an assignment, terminate the lease; the portal explains each case before you send.

    05How do I add a person to my lease?

    Fill out the Lease Addition form in your tenant portal: the person’s identity, arrival date and status, co-tenant signatory or simple occupant. The owner gives their approval, then the lease is updated.

    06What is a guarantor, and what do they commit to?

    The guarantor, or surety, commits to paying the rent and respecting the lease if the tenant does not. They choose how to show their ability to pay: credit check with their consent, or receipts and paid bills. They sign electronically; we never ask for their health insurance card.

    07How do I give feedback or file a complaint?

    Choose the category in the form: comment, complaint or administrative request. You receive an immediate acknowledgement of receipt, then we get back to you as soon as possible.

    08How do I see available units and book a showing?

    On our Units for rent page, with the rent and availability date of each unit. Cléo books your showing at any time.

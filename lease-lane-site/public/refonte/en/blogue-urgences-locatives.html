    Leak, heating, lock: what counts as an urgent repair?

    What is urgent according to the TAL, who acts and when, the minimum temperature in Québec City and the evidence to keep.

    01The short answer

    A repair is urgent when it is necessary to preserve the unit or keep it usable: a major leak, a heating failure in winter, a broken entrance lock, a blocked sewer[2]. The tenant first notifies the landlord. If the landlord cannot be reached or does not act in time, the tenant can have the essential work done and be reimbursed for reasonable expenses, on proof[1].

    02What is urgent, according to the TAL

    The Civil Code allows the tenant to act themselves when a repair is urgent and necessary to preserve the unit or its enjoyment[1]. The TAL gives examples[2]:

    • a major leak in the plumbing or roof;
    • faulty electrical wiring;
    • a blocked sewer;
    • a rotten balcony step or board that gives way;
    • a heating failure in winter, or a refusal to fill the oil tank;
    • a faulty entrance door lock.

    The TAL describes this remedy as an exceptional measure, to be used with caution and in good faith. The repair must be limited to the essentials, on a temporary basis[2].

    03Urgent or not?

    Examples of situations and the urgency level of a unit repair

    SituationUrgent?Who acts
    No heat in JanuaryYesThe landlord, without delay; failing that, the tenant does the essentials
    Active water damageYesShut off the water, notify the landlord, limit the damage
    Broken entrance door lockYesThe landlord; a lock cannot be changed without the agreement of both parties
    Dripping tapNoWritten notice to the landlord
    Spare appliance out of orderNoWritten notice to the landlord

    04Steps for the tenant

    1. Notify the landlord as soon as you notice the problem: the tenant must inform the landlord within a reasonable time of any significant defect[1].
    2. Try to reach them and note every attempt: date, time, method used.
    3. If they do not act in time, have the essential repair done, without waiting for the tribunal’s authorization[1].
    4. Keep the evidence: photos, invoices and replaced parts. You must account to the landlord and hand over the supporting documents[1].
    5. Get reimbursed: if you are not reimbursed, you can withhold the amount of the reasonable expenses from the rent[1][2].

    The landlord can step in at any time to finish the work themselves[1].

    05Heating in Québec City: 20 °C minimum

    In Québec City, the heating system must be able to maintain at least 20 °C in the unit, measured in the centre of the room one metre above the floor. Adjoining spaces must stay at 15 °C or higher, and the unit must be supplied with hot and cold water[3].

    These thresholds come from a Ville de Québec by-law. Elsewhere, check your municipality’s by-law.

    06Access to the unit in an emergency

    • The landlord must in principle give 24 hours’ notice to carry out work, except in an emergency[1].
    • The tenant can refuse access before 7 a.m. and after 7 p.m., except for urgent work[1].
    • No one can install or change a lock without the consent of both the landlord and the tenant[1].
    • For urgent repairs, the landlord can require the temporary vacating of the unit; the tenant keeps the right to a rent reduction or compensation[1].

    07And if it isn’t urgent?

    Send the landlord a written notice. If the landlord does not make the repair, the tenant must obtain the TAL’s authorization before doing the work themselves[1]. The tenant can also ask for a rent reduction or damages.

    Withholding rent for a non-urgent repair, without authorization, exposes the tenant to an action for unpaid rent. See unpaid rent.

    09Frequently asked questions

    01Can the landlord enter without notice?

    Yes, in an emergency (s. 1931 CCQ). For urgent work, the landlord can even enter before 7 a.m. or after 7 p.m. (s. 1933 CCQ).

    02Can I deduct the bill from my rent?

    Yes, for an urgent and necessary repair, if the landlord does not reimburse you, provided the expenses are reasonable and backed by supporting documents (ss. 1868 and 1869 CCQ).

    03What is the minimum temperature in Québec City?

    20 °C in the unit, under Ville de Québec by-law R.V.Q. 3021.

    04And if the repair isn’t urgent?

    Send a written notice. After that, the TAL can authorize you to do the work, reduce the rent or award damages (ss. 1863 and 1867 CCQ).

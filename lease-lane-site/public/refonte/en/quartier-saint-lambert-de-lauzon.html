    Town profile

    Renting an apartment in Saint-Lambert-de-Lauzon.

    Saint-Lambert-de-Lauzon covers nearly 110 km², on both sides of the Chaudière River. Its population is spread between the village, the wooded Parc-Boutin area to the west, and the Domaine-des-Érables and Place-des-Îles areas, along the Chaudière. The rest of the territory, fairly flat, is given over to agriculture and forest.

    At the 2021 census, the municipality had 6,817 residents. On Rue du Pont, the Centre de loisirs and Parc Alexis-Blanchet group together baseball, tennis and pickleball courts, skating rinks and a skatepark.

    • Housing

      Mostly single-family homes, spread between the village and the Domaine-des-Érables, Place-des-Îles and Parc-Boutin areas.

    • Nearby

      Centre de loisirs · École primaire du Bac · Autoroute Robert-Cliche

    • Good to know

      Autoroute Robert-Cliche (73) is the main road access. It leads to Lévis, to the north, then to Québec City by the Pierre-Laporte Bridge.

    History

    The history of Saint-Lambert-de-Lauzon.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1851

      Canonical erection of the parish

    2. 1855

      Creation of the parish municipality

    3. 1969

      Adoption of the name Saint-Lambert-de-Lauzon

    4. 2013

      Change of status to municipality

    5. 2017

      Parish merged with those of Lévis

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1828 – 1848

      //

      The municipality’s name recalls Pierre Lambert, the surveyor who drew up the plans of the seigneury of Lauzon in 1828, then those of the town of Aubigny, now part of Lévis, in 1848. Lauzon refers to the seigneury of Jean de Lauzon.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1851 – 1855

      //

      The Catholic parish of Saint-Lambert was canonically erected on May 30, 1851. The parish municipality of Saint-Lambert de Lauzon came into being on July 1, 1855.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1969 – 2013

      //

      On March 15, 1969, the parish municipality took the name Saint-Lambert-de-Lauzon. On June 22, 2013, it changed status and became a municipality.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 2012 – 2017

      //

      Population growth led to the expansion of the école primaire du Bac in 2012. In 2017, the Catholic parish was merged with parishes in Lévis, including those of Saint-Romuald and Charny, to form the Saint-Jean-l’Évangéliste parish.

    01Where is Saint-Lambert-de-Lauzon?

    In the MRC de La Nouvelle-Beauce, in Chaudière-Appalaches, about 20 km south of Québec City. The municipality borders Lévis to the north, and Saint-Henri and Saint-Isidore to the east.

    02Where does the name Saint-Lambert-de-Lauzon come from?

    It honours Pierre Lambert, the surveyor who drew up the plans of the seigneury of Lauzon in 1828. Lauzon recalls that seigneury, which belonged to Jean de Lauzon.

    03Which roads run through Saint-Lambert-de-Lauzon?

    Autoroute Robert-Cliche (73) runs through it from north to south. Routes 171 (Rue Bellevue) and 175 (Rue des Érables) also pass through, and Rue du Pont (Route 218) serves as the east-west axis.

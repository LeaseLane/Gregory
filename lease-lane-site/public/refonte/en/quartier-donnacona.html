    Town profile

    Renting an apartment in Donnacona.

    Donnacona is in the Portneuf RCM, on the north shore of the St. Lawrence River. The Jacques-Cartier River borders the northwest of the town up to its mouth, and the Rivière aux Pommes runs through the centre from east to west. Its neighbours are Pont-Rouge to the north, Cap-Santé to the west and Neuville to the east.

    In the 2021 census, the town had 7,436 residents over 37.3 km². Several regional organizations have offices there, including the Centre local d’emploi and the Carrefour jeunesse emploi de Portneuf. An industrial park extends along Autoroute 40.

    • Housing

      Mostly single-family houses, with duplexes and small apartment buildings near the main streets.

    • Nearby

      Rivière aux Pommes · Parc industriel de Donnacona · Pont-Rouge

    • Good to know

      Autoroute 40 connects Donnacona to Québec City to the east. A car remains the simplest way to get around Portneuf.

    History

    The history of Donnacona.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1672

      Seigneury of Pointe-aux-Écureuils

    2. 1912

      Arrival of Donnacona Paper

    3. 1915

      Founding of the municipality

    4. 1967

      Merger with Les Écureuils

    5. 2008

      Paper mill closes

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1535 – 1672

      //

      In 1672, Intendant Jean Talon granted the seigneury of Pointe-aux-Écureuils to Toussaint Toupin and his son Jean. The town today bears the name of Donnacona, an Iroquoian chief whose village of Stadacona Jacques Cartier described in 1535, near present-day Québec City.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1912 – 1915

      //

      In 1912, the arrival of the Donnacona Paper mill brought rapid growth. The municipality of Donnacona was founded in 1915, split off from Saint-Jean-Baptiste-des-Écureuils. At the height of activity, more than 1,500 men worked in the mills, and the New York Times was printed on their paper.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1924 – 1967

      //

      In 1924, a convent was built. Nuns taught girls at the école Sainte-Agnès, and brothers taught boys at the collège Sacré-Cœur. In 1967, Donnacona merged with the municipality of Les Écureuils. On July 24 of that year, General de Gaulle made the first stop of his Chemin du Roy journey there and gave a short speech.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 2008 – 2011

      //

      On January 31, 2008, the paper mill, by then Abitibi-Bowater, stopped production, and the closure became permanent. Demolition of the mill began in late March 2011. The town remains a service centre for the Portneuf region.

    01Where is Donnacona?

    In the Portneuf RCM, in the Capitale-Nationale region, on the north shore of the St. Lawrence, west of Québec City. You get there by Autoroute 40 or by the Chemin du Roy.

    02Where does the name Donnacona come from?

    From the Iroquoian chief Donnacona, who lived in the village of Stadacona, on the banks of the Saint-Charles River, when Jacques Cartier passed through in 1535. The residents are called Donnaconiens and Donnaconiennes.

    03What role did the paper mill play in Donnacona?

    The mill, opened in 1912, gave rise to the town and employed more than 1,500 men at its peak. It ceased production in 2008 and was demolished starting in 2011.

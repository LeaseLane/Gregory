    01The short answer

    Since January 2026, the increase is calculated with the TAL’s new method: a base rate applied to the rent, plus your unit’s share of the increase in taxes, insurance and major work. The 2027 rate will be published in January 2027; the notice then goes out 3 to 6 months before the lease ends.

    02What changes in 2027

    The TAL no longer publishes a grid of percentages by type of unit. A single method, simpler and more predictable, applies to all renewed leases. The calculator above uses its official rules; only the annual rate will change in January.

    03The calculation in 4 steps

    1. The base rate

    The current rent is multiplied by the annual rate published by the TAL.

    2. Taxes

    The increase in municipal and school taxes is divided among the units.

    3. Insurance

    Same principle for the building’s insurance premium.

    4. Major work

    A share of the cost of the work is added, spread over several years.

    04Deadlines for sending the notice

    Lease termNotice to send to the tenant
    12 months or moreBetween 3 and 6 months before the lease ends
    Less than 12 monthsBetween 1 and 2 months before the lease ends
    Indeterminate termBetween 1 and 2 months before the change
    The tenant has one month to respond

    With no response within the month, the tenant is deemed to have accepted the increase.

    05If the tenant refuses the increase

    The lease is renewed, and it is up to the landlord to ask the TAL to set the rent, within the month following the refusal. Keep the tax bills, the insurance policy and the work invoices: the tribunal asks for them.

    06Your questions about the 2027 rent increase

    01When will the 2027 rate be published?

    The TAL publishes the annual rate in January. The calculator switches to the 2027 rate as soon as it is published; until then, it uses the 2026 rate.

    02Can the tenant refuse the increase?

    Yes, within the month following the notice. The lease is then renewed and the landlord can ask the TAL to set the rent within the following month.

    03Can I increase the rent of a new unit?

    If the building was built, or its use changed, five years ago or less, and the mention is entered in the lease (section F), the tenant cannot ask the TAL to set the rent during that period.

    04What should I do if the building has had major work?

    Enter its cost under “Major work” and keep the invoices: the TAL asks for them if the tenant refuses.

    05Does the calculator replace legal advice?

    No. It is a general information tool. Check with the TAL before sending the notice.

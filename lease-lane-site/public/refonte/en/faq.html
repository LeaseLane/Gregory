    Offer and contract4

    01How is the management offer for my building put together?

    Each offer is prepared for your building, after a 30-minute call: number of doors, condition of the building, services wanted. You receive it in writing, with the transition schedule.

    02Which services are included in management?

    Rent collection, communications with tenants, coordination of maintenance, Cléo 24/7 and the monthly report. Re-renting, major work and files before the Tribunal administratif du logement (TAL) are specified in your offer.

    03How long is the contract, and how do I end it?

    The management contract lasts 12 months and renews. To not renew it, send written notice at least 30 days before its end; you can also end it at any time in writing. We then hand over leases, files and keys to the person you designate.

    04When and how are rents paid into my account?

    Rents collected are paid into your account at the frequency set out in your contract, after deduction of authorized expenses. Each payment is itemized in your owner portal.

    Transition2

    01How does the transfer work if I already have a manager?

    We first read your current contract to respect its notice period. We then collect leases, files, keys and histories, notify tenants and activate Cléo. Your first report arrives at the end of the first full month.

    02Do I keep control over important decisions?

    Yes. Tenant selection, work above your threshold, rent increases and any recourse to the TAL are submitted to you. You approve online, and the decision is logged in the file.

    Rentals2

    01How long does it take to rent a vacant unit?

    It depends on the area, the price and the season. Cléo answers prospective tenants at all times and books showings without waiting for office hours. The unit therefore stays empty for less time.

    02How are tenants screened, and how is their data protected?

    Each applicant chooses how to show their ability to pay: rent receipts or paid bills, references from previous landlords, a credit check with their consent, or a guarantor. We do not ask for income, employer or number of occupants, and the absence of a credit history is not a reason for refusal. Identification is shown at signing, with no copy kept, in accordance with Law 25.

    Day-to-day management4

    01Which reports will I receive, and how often?

    A monthly report, no later than the 15th of the month: rents due and received, arrears, vacant units, work, invoices and deadlines. The online dashboard shows the same figures continuously.

    02Who approves work, and above what amount?

    Below the approval threshold you set in Schedule B of your contract (no default amount), we hire the supplier and inform you. Above it, nothing is committed without your approval, except an emergency that threatens the safety of people or the building.

    03Do you manage plexes of two to five units?

    Yes. We manage plexes and all residential rental buildings, except divided co-ownership (condos) and short-term rentals. The service and the reports are the same, whatever the number of doors.

    04Which areas do you serve?

    We serve the greater Québec City region, within a 50 km radius: Québec City, the North Shore, the South Shore and Île d’Orléans. Write to us for an offer: we reply in less than 24 hours.

    Cléo1

    01How does Cléo respond to my tenants and prospective tenants, and when does a human take over?

    Cléo answers 24/7, French first, then in the language of the person it is talking to. It gives general information on the Civil Code and TAL rules that apply to both parties, books viewings and sorts emergencies. It hands over to a member of the team for an emergency, a dispute or formal notice, a repossession, an eviction or a termination, a harassment or discrimination complaint, or whenever asked. It announces every handoff: to whom, why and within what time frame.

    Rents and TAL7

    01How do you handle late payments and TAL files?

    A reminder goes out automatically on the first day of lateness, followed by a call from the team. If the delay continues, we prepare the file for the Tribunal administratif du logement, with your approval.

    02How is the annual rent increase set?

    We calculate the proposed increase using the method published each year by the TAL, then submit it to you. The notice of lease change goes out three to six months before the end of a twelve-month lease.

    03What does Bill 31 change for lease assignments?

    Since Bill 31, adopted in 2024, an owner who receives a notice of assignment can, rather than consent to it, terminate the lease. We present each option and its effects before replying to the tenant.

    04From when can I go to the TAL for unpaid rent?

    After more than three weeks of delay, you can ask the TAL to terminate the lease and recover the rent. We prepare the file and submit it to you before any filing.

    05How do I repossess a unit to live in it?

    Send a written notice at least six months before the end of a twelve-month lease. The tenant has one month to respond; if they refuse or do not respond, you must ask the TAL for authorization within the following month.

    06Can I require a security deposit?

    No. In Québec, only the first month’s rent can be requested in advance. No security deposit or postdated cheque can be required.

    07What is the TAL, the former Régie du logement?

    The Tribunal administratif du logement (TAL) replaced the Régie du logement on August 31, 2020. It decides disputes between tenants and landlords of residential units in Québec, and publishes the rent increase calculation method each year.

    Units and showings3

    01How do I see available units and book a showing?

    On our Units for rent page, with the rent and availability date of each unit. Cléo books your showing at any time.

    02Which documents are needed for a rental application?

    Your contact details, the unit you are applying for, the move-in date and the method you chose to show your ability to pay. Identification is shown when the lease is signed, with no copy kept. No deposit or postdated cheque is required.

    03Can Cléo answer in English or another language?

    Yes. Cléo answers first in French, then in the language of your message, including English. Official documents, such as the lease, remain in French.

    Work and emergencies2

    01How do I submit a work request and follow its progress?

    Submit the request in your tenant portal, with photos. You receive a tracking number and an appointment window, then a notice at each stage: received, scheduled, in progress, completed.

    02What is an emergency, and which number should I call?

    A major water leak, a heating failure in winter, a power outage, a door or lock that no longer closes, a faulty smoke detector. Call 418-473-3208 at any time; in case of danger, call 911.

    Rent2

    01How do I pay my rent?

    In your tenant portal or by transfer. You receive an automatic receipt as soon as the payment arrives.

    02When will I receive my Relevé 31?

    Your Relevé 31 is provided to you no later than the last day of February, for the previous year. It is used to claim the solidarity tax credit.

    Lease5

    01How do I assign my lease or sublet my unit?

    Send a written notice from your tenant portal, with the contact details of the proposed person. Since Bill 31, the owner can accept, refuse for a serious reason or, for an assignment, terminate the lease; the portal explains each case before you send.

    02How do I add a person to my lease?

    Fill out the Lease Addition form in your tenant portal: the person’s identity, arrival date and status, co-tenant signatory or simple occupant. The owner gives their approval, then the lease is updated.

    03What is a guarantor, and what do they commit to?

    The guarantor, or surety, commits to paying the rent and respecting the lease if the tenant does not. They choose how to show their ability to pay: credit check with their consent, or receipts and paid bills. They sign electronically; we never ask for their health insurance card.

    04How does lease renewal work?

    Your lease renews on the same terms. If the owner wants to change it, they send you a notice three to six months before the end of a twelve-month lease. You have one month after receiving the notice to reply in writing that you refuse the change or that you are leaving the unit; if you do not reply, you are deemed to have accepted it (art. 1945 C.C.Q.).

    05How does my move-out work?

    Give your notice to leave in your tenant portal, within the time limits set out in the lease. Cléo schedules re-rental showings with you, then we arrange the return of the keys.

    TAL and housing law4

    01What is the TAL, the former Régie du logement?

    The Tribunal administratif du logement (TAL) replaced the Régie du logement on August 31, 2020. It decides disputes between tenants and landlords of residential units in Québec, and publishes the rent increase calculation method each year.

    02Can I refuse a rent increase?

    Yes. You have one month after receiving the notice to refuse it in writing, and you stay in the unit. The landlord can then ask the TAL to set the rent, within the month following your refusal.

    03Can the landlord repossess my unit?

    Yes, to live there or house a close relative, by written notice at least six months before the end of a twelve-month lease. You have one month to respond; if you do not respond, you are deemed to refuse.

    04Can the landlord enter my unit?

    Yes. With 24 hours’ notice, they can check the condition of the unit, carry out work in it or show it to a buyer; showings take place between 9 a.m. and 9 p.m., work between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m. After your notice of non-renewal, they can show it to future tenants without notice, between 9 a.m. and 9 p.m. Major work requires written notice of at least 10 days, or 3 months if you must leave the unit for more than a week. You may refuse a showing if the owner or their representative cannot be present. In an emergency, no notice is required.

    Other requests2

    01How do I give feedback or file a complaint?

    Choose the category in the form: comment, complaint or administrative request. You receive an immediate acknowledgement of receipt, then we get back to you as soon as possible.

    02What should I do if there is a conflict with a neighbour?

    Write to us describing the situation, the dates and the effect on your unit. We contact the people involved and keep you informed of what follows.

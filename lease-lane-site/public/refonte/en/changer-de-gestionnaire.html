    04 · Frequently asked questions

    Questions about the transition.

    These answers come from the questions asked to Cléo. Our team validates and dates them before publication.

    Ask CléoAll questions
    01How does the transfer work if I already have a manager?

    We first read your current contract to respect its notice period. We then collect leases, files, keys and histories, notify tenants and activate Cléo. Your first report arrives at the end of the first full month.

    02How long is the contract, and how do I end it?

    The management contract lasts 12 months and renews. To not renew it, send written notice at least 30 days before its end; you can also end it at any time in writing. We then hand over leases, files and keys to the person you designate.

    03Do I keep control over the important decisions?

    Yes. Tenant selection, work above your threshold, rent increases and any recourse to the TAL are submitted to you. You approve online, and the decision is logged in the file.

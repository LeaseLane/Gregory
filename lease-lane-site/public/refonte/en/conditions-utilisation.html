    03What the site lets you do

    • Learn about our services for owners and our rates.
    • Browse available units and book a viewing.
    • Submit a request for a rental, an addition to the lease, an endorsement or a lease assignment.

    • Send us a comment, a complaint or an administrative request.

    04Cléo, our conversational agent

    • Cléo is an artificial intelligence (AI) agent, not a person. It introduces itself as such from the first message, and when a member of our team takes over, the conversation says so.
    • Like any artificial intelligence, Cléo can make mistakes: an answer may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Its answers are provided for information only; they are neither legal advice nor a commitment by LeaseLane. Check any important information with our team or an official source, such as the Tribunal administratif du logement. Only a signed lease and our written confirmations bind us.
    • Cléo makes no decision on a rental application: a member of our team reviews it.
    • Share with Cléo only the information useful for your request, never banking or health information.
    • Conversations are kept and processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
    • It is prohibited to try to divert Cléo from its role, to make it produce unlawful content or to circumvent its security measures.

    05Unit listings

    • Photos and plans may be illustrative, and dimensions are approximate.

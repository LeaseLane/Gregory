    Register my businessWrite to the team
    01Is registration free?

    Yes. You send your proof of insurance, your phone number and your email, at no cost.

    02Will my proof of insurance be public?

    No. It is sent to the LeaseLane team to process your registration and is never displayed publicly.

    03Why don’t you ask for my services right away?

    To keep registration short. Your services, your regions, your business description and your photos are filled in afterward in the platform.

    04Can I send a photo of my document?

    Yes. On a phone, choose “Take a photo”. Make sure the document is legible. Accepted formats: PDF, JPG and PNG, 10 MB maximum.

    05My email is already registered. What should I do?

    Log in to the platform or recover your access. There is no need to register a second time.

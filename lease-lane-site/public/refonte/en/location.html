    Law 25: only what is necessary

    Each piece of information requested is used to assess the application. It is kept for a limited time, then destroyed. The applicant can view and correct their file.

    Ask CléoAll questions
    01How long does it take to rent a vacant unit?

    It depends on the area, the price and the season. Cléo answers prospective tenants at all times and books showings without waiting for office hours. The unit therefore stays empty for less time.

    02How are tenants screened, and how is their data protected?

    Each applicant chooses how to show their ability to pay: rent receipts or paid bills, references from previous landlords, a credit check with their consent, or a guarantor. We do not ask for income, employer or number of occupants, and the absence of a credit history is not a reason for refusal. Identification is shown at signing, with no copy kept, in accordance with Law 25.

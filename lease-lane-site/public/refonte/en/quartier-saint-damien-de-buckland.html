    Town profile

    Rent an apartment in Saint-Damien-de-Buckland.

    Saint-Damien-de-Buckland, often called Saint-Damien, is in the mountains of southern Bellechasse, east of Saint-Lazare-de-Bellechasse and southwest of Armagh. The rivière aux Billots runs through the village and joins the rivière des Abénaquis at the southwest boundary. The Parc des Bâtisseurs, a 1 km wooded corridor, crosses the heart of the village.

    The municipality is home to the motherhouse of the Sisters of Notre-Dame du Perpétuel Secours, a congregation that spread to eleven countries from 1948. Plastics manufacturing provides many jobs. With the opening of a second CPE facility in 2025, Saint-Damien has 130 subsidized daycare spaces.

    • Housing

      Mostly single-family houses, with low-cost housing managed by the Office d’habitation Montmagny-Bellechasse. The municipality is preparing the second phase of a residential development.

    • Nearby

      Maison de la Culture de Bellechasse · Parc des Sœurs · Clinique médicale

    • Good to know

      Saint-Damien has a medical clinic, two CPE facilities and a secondary school. Route 279 runs through the municipality, and the MRC offers on-demand public transport to Lévis, Montmagny and Sainte-Marie.

    History

    The history of Saint-Damien-de-Buckland.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1859

      Creation of Buckland-et-Mailloux

    2. 1882

      Founding of the parish

    3. 1884

      End of construction of the church

    4. 1892

      Founding of the Sisters of Perpetual Help

    5. 1939

      Start of the company that would become IPL

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1806 – 1859

      //

      Settlers from neighbouring parishes moved in the late 1840s into the townships of Buckland and Mailloux. The united townships municipality of Buckland-et-Mailloux was created in 1859. The township of Buckland, proclaimed in 1806, is named after a locality in England.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1882 – 1884

      //

      Abbé Joseph-Onésime Brousseau founded the parish in 1882 and built, in the fall, a small sacristy that also served as his lodging. The church was built in 1883–1884 by Elzéar Métivier. During construction, a fire broke out in the bell tower, and the priest promised a place of pilgrimage to Saint Anne if the church was spared.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1892 – 1910

      //

      In 1892, Father Brousseau and Virginie Fournier founded the congregation of the Sisters of Notre-Dame du Perpétuel Secours. In 1905, a fire destroyed most of their buildings, and the motherhouse was rebuilt from 1906 to 1910. The Sainte-Anne-des-Montagnes chapel, destroyed by the same fire, was rebuilt and became a place of pilgrimage.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 1939 – 2014

      //

      In 1939, Joseph-Émile Métivier started the company that would become Les Industries Provinciales Ltée (IPL), a major name in plastics. The sisters opened a normal school in the early 1940s and left for the Dominican Republic in 1948, the start of their expansion abroad. The Parc des Bâtisseurs was developed from 2014.

    01Where is Saint-Damien-de-Buckland?

    In the south of the Bellechasse MRC, in Chaudière-Appalaches, between Saint-Lazare-de-Bellechasse to the west and Armagh to the northeast. Route 279 runs through the municipality.

    02Where does the name Saint-Damien-de-Buckland come from?

    Saint Damian is a martyred physician, celebrated with his brother Saint Cosmas. Buckland is the name of the township created in 1806, which recalls a locality in England.

    03What is the Sainte-Anne-des-Montagnes chapel?

    A pilgrimage chapel, also called the votive chapel, built at the request of Father Brousseau after a fire spared the church. Rebuilt after the 1905 fire, it is designated a heritage building by the municipality.

    Town profile

    Renting an apartment in Cap-Santé.

    Cap-Santé is the seat of the Portneuf RCM, in the Capitale-Nationale region. The town neighbours Portneuf, Saint-Basile, Pont-Rouge and Donnacona, and the Jacques-Cartier River marks its eastern boundary. Its old core, around the church, sits on a wooded terrace overlooking the river.

    In the 2021 census, Cap-Santé had 3,594 residents. The Sainte-Famille heritage site, classified in 1986, includes the church, the rectory, the cemetery and a section of the old road leading to the wharf.

    • Housing

      Mostly single-family houses, including several well-preserved 18th-century homes. Rental units are few: in 2021, about one household in nine was a tenant.

    • Nearby

      Parc régional de l’embouchure de la Jacques-Cartier · L’Escale du Roy · Promenade-Fleuve

    • Good to know

      On-street parking is prohibited from 11 p.m. to 7 a.m., from November 15 to April 1. The Corporation de transport régional de Portneuf provides two park-and-ride lots for carpoolers in Cap-Santé.

    History

    The history of Cap-Santé.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1679

      First parish register opened

    2. 1759

      Construction of Fort Jacques-Cartier

    3. 1767

      Completion of Église de la Sainte-Famille

    4. 1855

      Creation of the municipality of Sainte-Famille

    5. 2000

      Cap-Santé becomes a town

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1636 – 1679

      //

      In 1636, the Compagnie des Cent-Associés promised Jacques Le Neuf de la Poterie a concession on the heights of Cap-Santé and Portneuf. The first settlers arrived in 1675, and the parish’s first register was opened in 1679, the date used for the founding.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1681 – 1754

      //

      The seigneury of Portneuf became a barony in 1681. A chapel-rectory was built in 1709, then a first stone church from 1716 to 1718. Construction of the present church began in 1754.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1759 – 1767

      //

      In 1759, the church’s materials were used to build Fort Jacques-Cartier, at the mouth of the river. After the defeat on the Plains of Abraham, the French army spent the winter there, and Lévis left from there in the spring of 1760 for the Battle of Sainte-Foy. The residents then recovered the materials and finished the church in 1767.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 1855 – 2000

      //

      The municipality of the parish of Sainte-Famille was created in 1855. The church’s interior decor was carried out from 1859 to 1861 by Raphaël Giroux and the plasterer François Blouin. The municipality took the name Cap-Santé in 1979 and became a town in 2000.

    01Where is Cap-Santé?

    In the Portneuf RCM, on the north shore of the St. Lawrence, between Donnacona and Portneuf. The town is the seat of the RCM.

    02Where does the name Cap-Santé come from?

    Several explanations exist. According to the Commission de toponymie du Québec, it most likely comes from the Old French santeif, meaning wholesome, applied to the cape. A 1689 map already refers to the place as Cap Sante.

    03What is Fort Jacques-Cartier?

    A French fort built in 1759 at the mouth of the Jacques-Cartier River, after the fall of Québec City. The French army spent the winter there before the Battle of Sainte-Foy, and remains are still visible. The site and the neighbouring Allsopp manor have been classified since 1978.

    Neighbourhood profile

    Renting an apartment in Lebourgneuf.

    The neighbourhood covers the part of the Les Rivières borough north of Autoroute Félix-Leclerc and east of the Rivière Saint-Charles; the Rivière du Berger also crosses it. Lebourgneuf occupies its east side, with recent residential areas and large commercial and business zones along the Laurentienne and Félix-Leclerc highways.

    Its main routes are Avenue Chauveau and Boulevards Bastien, Lebourgneuf, Pierre-Bertrand and Robert-Bourassa. It has the Galeries de la Capitale and their Méga-parc, Parc Chauveau and Parc de l’Escarpement.

    • Housing

      Mostly apartment buildings and recent condos, often with an elevator and indoor parking.

    • Nearby

      Galeries de la Capitale · Autoroute Félix-Leclerc · Autoroute Laurentienne

    • Good to know

      A car-oriented area: ask whether a parking space is included and at what cost.

    History

    The history of Lebourgneuf.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    3. 1973

      Annexation to the City of Québec

    4. 1977

      Boulevard Lebourgneuf is named

    5. 1988

      The neighbourhood takes the name Lebourgneuf

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1795 – 1971

      //

      The founding of the parish of Saint-Ambroise-de-la-Jeune-Lorette in 1795 marked the start of settlement in the territory. As early as the end of the 17th century, Huron-Wendat people from Lorette, now L’Ancienne-Lorette, had settled there; their Wendake reserve is right next door today. In 1963, the southern part of the parish took the name Neufchâtel, and was integrated into Québec City in 1971.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1952 – 1988

      //

      The name Lebourgneuf combines the end of “Charlesbourg” and the start of “Neufchâtel,” the two sectors that frame it. The neighbourhood corresponds to the former municipality of Charlesbourg-Ouest, separated from Charlesbourg in 1952, annexed to Québec City in 1973 and renamed in 1988. Boulevard Lebourgneuf has carried this name since 1977.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1950

      //

      Until the 1950s, the area lived mostly from farming and had few residents. A few small country houses remain on Boulevard Bastien and Avenue Chauveau. It then urbanized rapidly, with recent residential areas and large zones of businesses and companies.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 2003

      //

      The current neighbourhood, Neufchâtel-Est–Lebourgneuf, was formed in 2003. It is part of the Les Rivières borough and lies near the geographic centre of Québec City.

    01Scope

    This policy explains how Solutions locatives LeaseLane inc., which carries on business under the name LeaseLane (“LeaseLane” or “we”), collects, uses, discloses, keeps and destroys personal information. It applies to the leaselane.ca website, the Cléo conversational agent, our forms and our property management services. It covers visitors, people looking for a unit, tenants, endorsers and owner clients.

    When we manage a building on behalf of an owner, some tenant information is also held by that owner [role to be confirmed]. This policy complies with Québec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, as amended by Law 25.

    02Person in charge of personal information protection

    The president is responsible for personal information protection at LeaseLane. For any question, or to exercise your rights, write to confidentialite@leaselane.ca.

    03Information we collect

    We collect only the information necessary for the stated purposes.

    Person concernedInformationWhy
    Site visitorIP address, device and browser, pages viewed, cookie choicesTo operate and secure the site; with your agreement, to measure its traffic
    Cléo userConversation content, contact details provided, language, unit of interest, viewing time slotTo respond, book viewings, carry out requested follow-ups, hand over to our team
    Prospective tenantIdentity, contact details, unit sought, move-in date, method chosen to demonstrate ability to pay: rent receipts or paid bills, references, or the result of the credit check if you consentTo review the rental application and enter into the lease
    TenantLease, rent payments, work requests (description, photos), communications, authorization to enter the unitTo manage the lease, maintenance and the rental relationship; to issue the Relevé 31
    EndorserIdentity, contact details, address, method chosen to demonstrate ability to pay: receipts or paid bills, or the result of the credit check if you consentTo review and record the endorsement
    Owner clientContact details, building information, banking information for paymentsTo provide and bill our services, to pay out the rents collected

    We do not ask for the social insurance number or the health insurance card to review a rental application or an endorsement. We verify identity without keeping a copy of the documents presented. Financial information and the result of a credit check are treated as sensitive information.

    04How we collect it

    • Directly from you: forms, conversations with Cléo, email, phone, viewings.
    • From third parties, with your consent: credit reporting agency, former landlords or other references you give us.
    • Automatically, according to your choices: cookies and similar technologies, described in our Cookie Policy.

    05Purposes of use

    We use your information only for the purposes announced at the time of collection:

    • respond to your requests and schedule viewings;
    • review rental and endorsement applications;
    • enter into, manage and renew leases;
    • process work requests and ensure the safety of buildings;
    • collect rents and pay the amounts owed to owners;
    • meet our legal obligations, for example issuing the Relevé 31 or responding to the Tribunal administratif du logement;
    • improve our services, using aggregated or anonymized data where possible;
    • send you promotional information, only if you have consented; you can unsubscribe at any time.

    Any new purpose requires your consent, except as provided by law.

    06Consent

    • We obtain your consent before collecting, using or disclosing your information, except as provided by law.
    • For sensitive information, such as the result of a credit check, this consent is express: a separate box, never pre-checked.
    • You can withdraw your consent at any time, subject to our legal or contractual obligations. We then explain the consequences, for example the inability to review a rental application.
    • The consent of a person under 14 is given by the person with parental authority.

    07Cléo and artificial intelligence

    • Cléo is an artificial intelligence agent, and it introduces itself as such at the start of every conversation.
    • Conversations are recorded to follow up on your requests, allow handoff to our team and improve the service. Only authorized members of our team can view them.
    • Cléo runs on a technology provider (Tonia, tonia.ca, a platform that relies on several AI models), which processes conversations on our behalf under a written agreement. Your conversations are not used to train this provider’s models [to be confirmed].
    • No decision based exclusively on automated processing is made about you. If that changed, we would inform you no later than the time of the decision. You could then learn the information and reasons behind it, and ask for a member of our team to review it.

    08Disclosure to third parties

    We do not sell your personal information. We disclose it only when necessary, to the following:

    • the owner of the building concerned, for whom we act as manager;
    • our service providers (hosting, management software, payment, chat), bound by a written agreement that protects confidentiality;
    • a credit reporting agency, with your consent;
    • contractors in charge of the work, for only the information that is useful, such as name, unit number and availability;
    • authorities, when the law requires it, for example Revenu Québec or the Tribunal administratif du logement;
    • another business, as part of a commercial transaction and under the conditions set by law.

    09Information processed outside Québec

    Some providers may host or process information outside Québec (notably in the United States). Before any disclosure outside Québec, we carry out a privacy impact assessment. We also ensure, through a written agreement, that the information receives adequate protection.

    10Retention and destruction

    We keep information for as long as necessary for the stated purposes, or as long as required by law, then we securely destroy or anonymize it.

    InformationRetention period
    Unsuccessful rental application90 days after the decision
    Tenant file and leaseLease term, then 3 years
    Conversations with CléoLease term, then 3 years; inactive prospective tenants: 24 months
    Audience measurement data14 months
    Owners’ accounting records6 years after the end of the relevant tax year

    11Security and confidentiality incidents

    • We limit access to those who need it, encrypt data in transit and at rest, log access, require two-factor authentication for our team and train them.
    • In the event of a confidentiality incident, we quickly take steps to reduce the risk of harm.
    • If the incident presents a risk of serious harm, we notify the Commission d’accès à l’information and the people concerned. Each incident is recorded in a register.

    12Your rights

    At any time, you can:

    • find out what information we hold about you and get a copy;
    • have inaccurate, incomplete or equivocal information corrected;
    • withdraw your consent;
    • ask that the dissemination of information cease, or that a hyperlink leading to it be de-indexed, where the law permits;
    • receive the computerized information you provided to us in a structured, commonly used technological format, or have it sent to a person of your choice;
    • be informed of any technology that allows you to be identified, located or profiled, and of the means to deactivate these functions.

    To exercise these rights, write to our person in charge (section 2); we may ask you to confirm your identity. We respond in writing within 30 days of receiving the request. Access is free; reasonable fees may apply for transcription, reproduction or transmission, and we notify you of them first.

    13Cookies

    Our cookies and similar technologies are described in our Cookie Policy, available at leaselane.ca/temoins. Only essential cookies are active without your agreement.

    14Minors

    The site is not intended for people under 14. We do not knowingly collect their information without the consent of the person with parental authority.

    15Complaints

    If you believe we have not respected your rights, write to our person in charge describing the situation, the information concerned and the relevant dates. We acknowledge receipt of your complaint and respond in writing within 30 days. If the response does not satisfy you, you can contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

    16Changes to the policy

    We update this policy when our practices change; the date of the last update appears at the top of the page. If a change affects how we use your information, we inform you before it applies and, if needed, we ask for your consent again.

    17Contact us

    Write to the president, person in charge of personal information protection, Solutions locatives LeaseLane inc. (LeaseLane), confidentialite@leaselane.ca.

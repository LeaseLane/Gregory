    Town profile

    Renting an apartment in Lac-Sergent.

    Lac-Sergent is part of the Portneuf MRC, in the Capitale-Nationale region. The town surrounds Lac Sergent, the smallest of the area’s three large resort lakes, along with Lac Saint-Joseph and Lac Sept-Îles. The lake is surrounded by wooded mountains rising above 300 m in elevation.

    At the 2021 census, Lac-Sergent had 541 residents. The lake normally freezes from November to April, and its shores are lined with many homes and cottages.

    • Housing

      Almost exclusively detached houses and cottages around the lake. Rental units are very rare there: in 2021, nearly all households were owners.

    • Nearby

      Lac Saint-Joseph · Lac Sept-Îles · Saint-Raymond

    • Good to know

      Saint-Raymond is about 10 km away and Québec City about 40 km. The Vélopiste Jacques-Cartier/Portneuf runs through the town and joins the Corridor des Cheminots toward Québec City.

    History

    The history of Lac-Sergent.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1829

      //

      The lake’s name was in use before 1829. According to the most widely held explanation, it recalls an old soldier who held the rank of sergeant and lived on its shores. Another theory sees it as a corruption of serre-joint (clamp), a carpenter’s tool whose shape the lake resembles.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1896

      //

      The railway between Québec City and Lac-Saint-Jean opened the area to summer vacationing. The first summer residents, mostly from Québec City, arrived in 1896 and settled in the southwest part of the lake, near the two stations, Lake Sergent Station and Lake Sergent East Station.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1908 – 1921

      //

      The Chapelle Notre-Dame-de-la-Paix was blessed on June 28, 1908, and a summer post office opened in 1909. On February 25, 1921, the town was incorporated from territories detached from Saint-Raymond and Sainte-Catherine-de-Fossambault.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 1961 – 1998

      //

      Passenger trains between Québec City and Saint-Raymond stopped in 1961. The Club de canotage du Lac-Sergent was founded in 1989. After the railway was abandoned, its route became the Vélopiste Jacques-Cartier/Portneuf, opened to the public in 1998.

    01Where is Lac-Sergent?

    In the Portneuf MRC, about 40 km from Québec City and 10 km from Saint-Raymond. The town surrounds Lac Sergent, between Saint-Raymond and Sainte-Catherine-de-la-Jacques-Cartier.

    02Where does the name Lac-Sergent come from?

    The most widely held version refers to an old soldier, a former sergeant, who lived by the lake. Another theory links it to the word serre-joint (clamp), a tool whose shape the lake is said to resemble.

    03Can you cycle in Lac-Sergent?

    Yes. The Vélopiste Jacques-Cartier/Portneuf, built on a former railway line, runs through the town. At 68 km long, it links Saint-Gabriel-de-Valcartier to Rivière-à-Pierre.

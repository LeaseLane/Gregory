    Town profile

    Renting an apartment in Fossambault-sur-le-Lac.

    Fossambault-sur-le-Lac is part of the La Jacques-Cartier RCM, in the Capitale-Nationale region. The town occupies the lands around the southern part of Lac Saint-Joseph, a glacial lake about thirty kilometres from Québec City. The Rivière aux Pins crosses the north of its territory before flowing into the lake.

    In the 2021 census, the town had 2,327 residents. Lac Saint-Joseph covers 11.31 km² and is 8.1 km long from north to south. The Duchesnay resort surrounds it to the west, north and east.

    • Housing

      Mostly houses and cottages, including several early 20th-century vacation buildings. Some summer residences are now lived in year-round.

    • Nearby

      Station touristique Duchesnay · Piste cyclable Jacques-Cartier/Portneuf · Sainte-Catherine-de-la-Jacques-Cartier

    • Good to know

      Fossambault borders Sainte-Catherine-de-la-Jacques-Cartier to the southwest and Shannon to the northeast. Since 1973, a water and sewer system has served most of the lake’s residences.

    History

    The history of Fossambault-sur-le-Lac.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1896

      Start of cottage life

    2. 1902

      Chapelle Saint-Joseph-du-Lac built

    3. 1934

      Thomas Maher buys the lake lands

    4. 1949

      Incorporation of the village

    5. 1974

      Fossambault becomes a town

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1817 – 1833

      //

      In the 19th century, only a few Irish farmers lived around the lake. In 1833, about thirty Irish families, who had arrived from Connecticut in 1817, occupied the southeastern area of the lake. The town’s name pays tribute to Jacques Nau de La Boissière et de Fossambault, King’s counsellor and treasurer of finances in Berry.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1869 – 1902

      //

      The railway reached the region in 1869, and cottage life began in 1896, when the notary Cyprien Labrecque had a residence built. Notaries, doctors and merchants from Québec City followed. The Saint-Joseph-du-Lac chapel, built entirely of wood, was built in 1902.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1934

      //

      In 1934, the businessman Thomas Maher bought lands around the lake from the Consolidated Paper Corporation. He divided them into lots for vacationers and required, in the sales contracts, houses suited to the landscape.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 1949 – 1974

      //

      The village of Fossambault-sur-le-Lac was incorporated on March 10, 1949, with Jules Gingras as its first mayor. The Notre-Dame-de-Liesse chapel was erected in 1950, and the municipality gained town status in 1974.

    Town profile

    Renting an apartment in Saint-Raymond.

    Saint-Raymond extends on both sides of the Sainte-Anne River, in the heart of the MRC de Portneuf. The urban core, in the hollow of the valley, has about 3,000 residents. The rest of the population lives in hamlets such as Bourg-Louis and Chute-Panet, residential neighbourhoods and vacation areas around the lakes.

    At the 2021 census, the town had 11,108 residents over 693.6 km². The Centre multifonctionnel Rolland-Dion, with its six halls, hosts shows and artists. Every July, the Festival forestier de Saint-Raymond brings the town to life.

    • Housing

      Single-family homes in the centre and in residential neighbourhoods, lakeside residences and a few apartment buildings near the centre.

    • Nearby

      Centre multifonctionnel Rolland-Dion · Sainte-Anne River · Laurentides Wildlife Reserve

    • Good to know

      The territory covers 693.6 km² and several areas are far from the centre. A car is almost essential, especially in winter.

    History

    The history of Saint-Raymond.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1830

      Arrival of settlers from L’Ancienne-Lorette

    2. 1842

      Founding of the Saint-Raymond-Nonnat parish

    3. 1898

      Creation of the village of Saint-Raymond

    4. 1905

      Birth of the hamlet of Chute-Panet

    5. 1995

      Village and parish reunited

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · Chapter 01

      //

      In the 17th and 18th centuries, after their defeat by the Iroquois, the Wendat settled in the seigneury of Bourg-Louis and the neighbouring townships. They hunted, fished and gathered berries there.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1830 – 1845

      //

      Settlers from L’Ancienne-Lorette established themselves on the territory as early as 1830. The Catholic parish of Saint-Raymond-Nonnat was founded around 1842. Its name honours Raymond Nonnatus, a Catalan cardinal. The civil parish was erected in 1845.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1898 – 1958

      //

      In 1898, the village split from the parish municipality. In 1905, the hamlet of Chute-Panet formed around the facilities of the Compagnie de papier Saint-Raymond. In 1958, the parish dropped the “Nonnat” particle from its name.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 1995

      //

      On March 29, 1995, the village and the parish came together to form the town of Saint-Raymond. The former railway that led to Rivière-à-Pierre and Lac-Saint-Jean has become a cycling trail. Wood and leather remain at the heart of the local economy.

    01Where is Saint-Raymond?

    In the centre of the MRC de Portneuf, in the Capitale-Nationale region, 25 km north of Donnacona. It is sometimes called Saint-Raymond-de-Portneuf.

    02What is there to do in the Bras-du-Nord valley?

    The valley is about 10 km north of the town. You can canoe, kayak, hike and mountain bike there. A local co-operative manages the site.

    03Why is Saint-Raymond called the “Car Town”?

    The nickname comes from the large number of car dealerships in the town.

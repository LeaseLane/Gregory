    Town profile

    Renting an apartment in Sainte-Hénédine.

    Sainte-Hénédine is a parish municipality in the La Nouvelle-Beauce RCM, between the Chaudière River to the west and the Etchemin River to the east. It neighbours Saint-Isidore and is 14 km from Saint-Anselme. The Le Bras River, a tributary of the Etchemin, crosses the territory, and Route 275 passes through the village.

    Agriculture dominates, with several hog farmers and dairy producers. The population has exceeded 1,000 for about a century and a half. The church site, with its 1940 presbytery and its cemetery laid out in 1885, is designated a heritage site by the municipality.

    • Housing

      Mostly single-family homes and farms. The village also has a few plexes and new rental units.

    • Nearby

      Bibliothèque La Détente · École La Découverte · Municipal bike path

    • Good to know

      Route 275 connects the village to Saint-Isidore, where the Autoroute Robert-Cliche (A-73) passes. A public transit service is available to residents.

    History

    The history of Sainte-Hénédine.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1852

      Canonical erection of the parish

    2. 1855

      Creation of the parish municipality

    3. 1893

      First waterworks, with wooden pipes

    4. 1912

      Present church blessed

    5. 1925

      Arrival of electricity in the village

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · Chapter 01

      //

      The territory was part of the seigneuries of Sainte-Marie and Jolliet, owned by the Taschereau family. The first settlers, from Sainte-Marie, Saint-Henri, Saint-Gervais and Sainte-Claire, settled along the route Sainte-Thérèse, which the Taschereau family had laid out to populate the hinterland.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1850 – 1855

      //

      Around 1850, about a thousand people lived there and asked for their own parish. Archbishop Pierre-Flavien Turgeon canonically erected it on March 20, 1852, from parts of Sainte-Marguerite, Sainte-Claire and Sainte-Marie. The name pays tribute to Catherine-Hénédine Dionne, widow of the seigneur Pierre-Elzéar Taschereau, who contributed to the building of the church. The parish municipality was created in 1855.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1870 – 1967

      //

      The Lévis and Kennebec railway crossed the parish in the 1870s, and the station stayed open until about 1965. In 1893, Joseph Mercier built a first waterworks with wooden pipes, which served the village until 1967.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 1910 – 1933

      //

      On October 1, 1910, lightning set fire to the first church, while the new one was already under construction. The current Romanesque church was blessed in 1912. Electricity arrived in the village in 1925, and a bus line connected the parish to Lévis and Québec City by Route 275 starting in 1933.

    01Where is Sainte-Hénédine?

    On the South Shore, in the La Nouvelle-Beauce RCM in Chaudière-Appalaches, between the Chaudière and Etchemin rivers. The village neighbours Saint-Isidore and is 14 km from Saint-Anselme.

    02Where does the name Sainte-Hénédine come from?

    It pays tribute to Catherine-Hénédine Dionne, widow of the seigneur Pierre-Elzéar Taschereau and a benefactor of the church. Its patron saint, Hénédine, was a 2nd-century martyr from Sardinia. It is also called Sainte-Hénédine-de-Dorchester.

    03What is there to see in Sainte-Hénédine?

    The church, built from 1910 to 1912, with its two bell towers and its octagonal false dome. Together with the presbytery, the tithe barn and the cemetery, it forms a heritage site designated by the municipality.

    Neighbourhood profile

    Rent an apartment in Saint-Émile.

    Saint-Émile is surrounded by woods; a power line and a golf course border it to the south. Avenue Lapierre runs through it from north to south and Rue de la Faune from east to west; Rue Vézina completes its main arteries. The terrain varies between 110 and 230 m, dominated by Le Rocher, and the ruisseau de la Montagne Saint-Charles runs along the east of the neighbourhood.

    In the 2016 census, 17.7% of residents were tenants and the average age was 36.1, compared with 43.2 for the city as a whole. The neighbourhood has many parks, including Grande-Oasis, Réal-Cloutier and du Millénaire. It is also home to the Centre communautaire Saint-Émile and Saint-Émile church, built in 1926–1927.

    • Housing

    History

    The history of Saint-Émile.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1647

      Land granted to Robert Giffard

    2. 1662

      Route Saint-Romain opens

    3. 1925

      Parish of Saint-Émile established

    4. 1929

      Municipality of Saint-Émile founded

    5. 2002

      Merger with the City of Québec

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1647

      //

      The territory of Saint-Émile is part of a grant made to Robert Giffard in 1647. That same year, he gave these lands to the Augustinian nuns of the Hôtel-Dieu de Québec, as the dowry of his daughter Françoise, who entered as a novice. The nuns named their domain the Saint-Ignace seigneury, but at first settled no one there, for fear of the Iroquois.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1662 – 1794

      //

      In 1662, a road called Saint-Romain was opened and a village of the same name formed, attached to the parish of Charlesbourg. In 1794, the parish of Saint-Ambroise was founded at Jeune-Lorette, now Loretteville. Saint-Romain was part of it, as was the village of Saint-Ignace, located on what is now Rue de la Faune.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1904 – 1975

      //

      The name Saint-Émile appeared in 1904, with the opening of the Saint-Émile-de-Québec post office. The parish was established on August 14, 1925, and the church, designed by architect Émile-Georges Rousseau, was built in 1926–1927. The municipality of Saint-Émile was founded in 1929 and celebrated the 50th anniversary of its founding in 1975.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 1996 – 2016

      //

      The municipality had 10,694 residents in 2000. During the municipal reorganizations of 2002, it was merged with Québec City and included in the La Haute-Saint-Charles borough. The neighbourhood has continued to grow: between 1996 and 2016, its population rose from 9,885 to 15,640, an increase of 58.2%.

    The name appeared in 1904 with the Saint-Émile-de-Québec post office. The parish, established in 1925, was placed under the patronage of Saint Emilian, a 3rd-century martyr of Carthage.

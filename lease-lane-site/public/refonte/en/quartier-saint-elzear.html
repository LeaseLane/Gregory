    Town profile

    Rent an apartment in Saint-Elzéar.

    Saint-Elzéar is set on the banks of the Beaurivage River, and the Nadeau and Savoie rivers also water its territory. The village crowns a hill, and the church grounds offer a view of the Chaudière valley and the Appalachians. It is 7 km from Sainte-Marie, near Saint-Bernard.

    The territory covers 85.12 km². Two-thirds of the population live in the village, and one-third live in a farming setting. Hog farming and the dairy industry are among the foundations of the economy, along with an industrial park and about a hundred businesses.

    • Housing

      Mostly single-family houses in the village, with a few duplexes and small buildings. One-third of the population lives in a farming setting.

    • Nearby

      Sentiers du Mont-Cosmos · Bibliothèque municipale · Sainte-Marie, 7 km away

    • Good to know

      The village is 7 km from Sainte-Marie and 56 km from Québec City. Route 216 runs through the municipality.

    History

    The history of Saint-Elzéar.

    The key dates, then the story in four chapters.

    1. 1835

      Canonical erection of the parish

    2. 1854

      End of construction of the church

    3. 1855

      Creation of the parish municipality

    4. 1960

      Church and sacristy classified

    5. 1994

      Village and parish amalgamate

    1. 01

      Chapter 01 · 1835 – 1845

      //

      The territory was detached from the Sainte-Marie seigneury, long owned by the Taschereau de Linière family. The parish of Saint-Elzéar-de-Linière was established canonically in 1835. The first settlers arrived mostly around 1845, from Île d’Orléans, Beauport, Château-Richer and Sainte-Marie, and Irish families joined them.

    2. 02

      Chapter 02 · 1809 – 1871

      //

      The name honours Henri-Elzéar Juchereau Duchesnay (1809–1871), seigneur of Sainte-Marie at the time, a lawyer and later a senator. A municipality of Saint-Elzéar was created in 1845 and abolished two years later. The post office opened in 1853, and the parish municipality came into being in 1855.

    3. 03

      Chapter 03 · 1852 – 1960

      //

      Residents built the stone church from 1852 to 1854. Léandre Parent did the interior décor from 1855 to 1860, and Ferdinand Villeneuve added an altarpiece in 1893–1894. The church and sacristy were classified as historic monuments in 1960.

    4. 04

      Chapter 04 · 1955 – 2024

      //

      In 1955, the village separated from the parish municipality. The two entities amalgamated on November 30, 1994 under the name Saint-Elzéar. The Mont-Cosmos observatory, linked to Université Laval, acquired a new telescope in 2024.

    01Where is Saint-Elzéar?

    On the South Shore, in the La Nouvelle-Beauce MRC in Chaudière-Appalaches, 7 km from Sainte-Marie and 56 km from Québec City. The village is built on a hill, near the Beaurivage River.

    02Where does the name Saint-Elzéar come from?

    It honours Henri-Elzéar Juchereau Duchesnay (1809–1871), seigneur of Sainte-Marie, legislative councillor and senator. The parish was first called Saint-Elzéar-de-Linière, recalling the Taschereau de Linière family.

    03What is there to do in Saint-Elzéar?

    Visit the classified church, built from 1852 to 1854, observe the sky at the Mont-Cosmos observatory or walk its trails. The Fêtes de chez nous take place every year on Labour Day weekend.

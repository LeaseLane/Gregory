    Glossary of the residential lease in Québec.

    The words you use, then the Civil Code or TAL term. General legal information, not legal advice.

    To be validated by LeaseLane before publication (page not indexed). Each definition repeats word for word an answer already published on the site, except “3 ½, 4 ½” (general definition, new).

    1. “Régie du logement”

      Civil Code or TAL termTribunal administratif du logement (TAL)

      The Tribunal administratif du logement (TAL) replaced the Régie du logement on August 31, 2020. It decides disputes between tenants and landlords of residential units in Québec, and publishes the rent increase calculation method each year.

      Source: FAQ

    2. “Rent increase”

      Civil Code or TAL termNotice of lease change, rent determination

      Your lease renews on the same terms. If the owner wants to change it, they send you a notice three to six months before the end of a twelve-month lease. You have one month after receiving the notice to reply in writing that you refuse the change or that you are leaving the unit; if you do not reply, you are deemed to have accepted it (art. 1945 C.C.Q.). Yes. You have one month after receiving the notice to refuse it in writing, and you stay in the unit. The owner can then ask the TAL to set the rent, within the month following your refusal.

      Source: Rent increase 2026

    3. “Eviction”

      Civil Code or TAL termRepossession, termination, eviction

      Yes, to live in it or house a close relative, by written notice at least six months before the end of a twelve-month lease. You have one month to reply; if you do not reply, you are deemed to refuse. After more than three weeks of lateness, you can ask the TAL for termination of the lease and recovery of the rent. We prepare the file and submit it to you before any filing.

      Source: FAQ

    4. “Unpaid rent, late payment”

      Civil Code or TAL termRecovery, application to the TAL

      After more than three weeks of delay, you can ask the TAL to terminate the lease and recover the rent. We prepare the file and submit it to you before any filing.

      Source: FAQ

    5. “Deposit, security deposit”

      Civil Code or TAL termProhibited in Québec (art. 1904 C.C.Q.)

      In Québec, only the first month’s rent can be required in advance (C.C.Q., art. 1904). Security deposits, key deposits, imposed postdated cheques and application fees are prohibited; a sum collected this way is refundable with interest.

      Source: Cléo

    6. “Transfer my lease, leave, move”

      Civil Code or TAL termLease assignment, sublease, notice to leave

      Send a written notice from your tenant portal, with the contact details of the proposed person. Since Bill 31, the owner can accept, refuse for a serious reason or, for an assignment, terminate the lease; the portal explains each case before you send. Give your notice to leave in your tenant portal, within the time limits set out in the lease. Cléo schedules re-rental showings with you, then we arrange the return of the keys.

      Source: Tenant service

    7. “Roommate, spouse”

      Civil Code or TAL termAddition of a person to the lease

      Fill out the Lease Addition form in your tenant portal: the person’s identity, arrival date and status, co-tenant signatory or simple occupant. The owner gives their approval, then the lease is updated.

      Source: Tenant service

    8. “Leak, water damage, toilet, heating”

      Civil Code or TAL termWork request, emergency

      A major water leak, a heating failure in winter, a power outage, a door or lock that no longer closes, a faulty smoke detector. Call 418-473-3208 at any time; in case of danger, call 911. Submit the request in your tenant portal, with photos. You receive a tracking number and an appointment window, then a notice at each stage: received, scheduled, in progress, completed.

      Source: 24/7 emergency

    9. “3 ½, 4 ½, apartment”

      Civil Code or TAL termDwelling

      In Québec, a unit is often described by its number of rooms, with the bathroom counting as a half: a 4 ½ has four rooms and a bathroom.

      Source: Units for rent

    10. “Quote, price, rate”

      Civil Code or TAL termService offer

      Each offer is prepared for your building, after a 30-minute call: number of doors, condition of the building, services wanted. You receive it in writing, with the transition schedule.

      Source: Service offer
